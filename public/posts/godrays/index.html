About
me

Currently, I am a student at The Game Assembly, looking for internship. The aspects that makes me love games as a developer are the creativity it allows a programmer and its interactive nature. What peaks my interest the most is engine work: architecture, systems, pipelines, data flow, algorithms and more. I find graphics programming extra intriguing because of this. I also find gameplay intriguing, with the challenge of bridging the gap between the interactive world and the hands of the player.

I began coding in high school in C# and after graduation C and C++ caught my interest because of its wide use and the wealth of online resources, along with the control it offers. To this day I remember the amazement I felt when I found out about pointer casts.
